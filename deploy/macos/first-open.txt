CheeseWAF for macOS

1. Drag CheeseWAF into Applications.
2. If macOS says the app is damaged or cannot be opened, that is Gatekeeper
   blocking an unsigned PreTest build. Run "Fix Gatekeeper.command" on this
   disk, or in Terminal:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/CheeseWAF.app
   open /Applications/CheeseWAF.app

3. CheeseWAF starts a local controller and opens http://127.0.0.1:17943/

macOS 安装说明

1. 把 CheeseWAF 拖进「应用程序」。
2. 若提示已损坏或无法打开，是系统拦截了未公证的 PreTest 包。
   双击本盘里的「Fix Gatekeeper.command」，或在终端执行：

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/CheeseWAF.app
   open /Applications/CheeseWAF.app

3. 程序会启动本机控制台：http://127.0.0.1:17943/
